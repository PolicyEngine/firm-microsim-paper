Value-added VAT notch: model A (inputs as a real cost) vs model B
========================================================================
TWO PROFIT FUNCTIONS (same iso-elastic C, different meaning of C)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  tau = 0.20 statutory; delta = sector deductible-input share of
  turnover; value-added share = (1 - delta); d = delta*y inputs.

  MODEL B (existing paper / deductible_model.py):
    Unregistered:  pi_B = y                       - C(y;n,e)
    Registered:    pi_B = (1 - tau*(1-delta))*y   - C(y;n,e)
    -> C is TOTAL turnover cost; inputs are NOT separately netted.

  MODEL A (this module):
    Unregistered:  pi_A = (1-delta)*y             - C(y;n,e)
    Registered:    pi_A = (1-delta)*(1-tau)*y     - C(y;n,e)
    -> C is OWN-FACTOR (NON-INPUT) cost; inputs d=delta*y are a
       real cost subtracted from turnover.

  C(y;n,e) = (n/(1+1/e))*(y/n)**(1+1/e) is the SAME FUNCTION in both;
  only its economic INTERPRETATION differs. A and B coincide only at
  delta=0; in general w_B - w_A = delta exactly.

  Registered wedge   w_A=(1-delta)(1-tau)   w_B=1-tau(1-delta)
  Registered optimum y*_A=n[(1-delta)(1-tau)]**e   y*_B=n[1-tau(1-delta)]**e
  Notch jump at T*   Delta_A = Delta_B = tau*(1-delta)*T*  (IDENTICAL)
  Dominated width    a_A = T*tau/(1-tau) (delta-INDEPENDENT)
                     a_B = T*tau(1-delta)/(1-tau(1-delta)) (shrinks)

VERDICT (defensibility -- neither is identified by the data)
========================================================================
Both readings use the IDENTICAL iso-elastic cost form; they differ
only in whether deductible input spend d=delta*y is subtracted as a
real cost (A) or folded into total cost C (B). The data cannot tell
them apart -- it is a MODELLING CONVENTION, not an identified fact.

* Model B is INTERNALLY CONSISTENT with the rest of the paper: its
  turnover-based ability recovery and calibration to net-VAT-to-
  turnover data both presume the 'C = total turnover cost' reading,
  and its sector-shrinking dominated region follows from the same
  effective wedge tau*(1-delta) used everywhere else.
* Model A is the more LITERAL 'VAT on value added with inputs as a
  real cost' statement. Notably it REPRODUCES the paper's headline
  £21,250 Kleven-Waseem dominated region for EVERY sector (the
  (1-delta) cancels), whereas B makes that region sector-specific.
* Both agree EXACTLY on the notch jump tau*(1-delta)*T*. They differ
  on the retained wedge (by exactly delta), hence on recovered
  abilities (A higher) and on reform responsiveness (A larger,
  reforms relatively cheaper).

Recommendation: state the convention explicitly. B is defensible and
coherent with the paper as written; A is an equally defensible
alternative that happens to restore the canonical £21,250 figure.
