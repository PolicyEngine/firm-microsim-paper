Explicit value-added (deductible) reformulation of the VAT notch
========================================================================
MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Statutory rate tau = 0.20; sector deductible share delta in [0,1).
  Value added = turnover - deductibles = (1 - delta)*y.
  Unregistered (y <  T*):  pi = y                       - C(y;n,e)
  Registered   (y >= T*):  pi = (1 - tau*(1-delta))*y   - C(y;n,e)
  C(y;n,e) = (n/(1+1/e))*(y/n)**(1+1/e),  c'(y) = (y/n)**(1/e).

  Effective wedge      tau_eff(delta) = tau*(1-delta)
  Registered optimum   y* = n*(1 - tau*(1-delta))**e
  Notch jump at T*     tau_eff*T*
  Dominated width      a(delta) = T**tau_eff/(1-tau_eff)

VERDICT
========================================================================
The explicit value-added reformulation is SOUND. Levying tau on
value added (turnover - deductibles) instead of on the whole
turnover yields a registered net rate (1 - tau*(1-delta)) whose
iso-elastic optimum y* = n*(1 - tau*(1-delta))**e is recovered by the
numerical optimisers to better than 1e-4 relative error, with a
single strictly-concave interior maximum. It is EXACTLY EQUIVALENT to
the existing (1 - tau0)*y simulator with tau0 = tau*(1-delta): the
optimum, the dominated-region width, the registered profit and the
marginal buncher all coincide to machine precision (PASS above).

WHAT IT CHANGES relative to the implicit form: the wedge is no longer
an opaque ~3% number but tau x (value-added share), grounded in the
sector deductible share delta. This makes the notch and the dominated
region SECTOR-SPECIFIC. High-deductible / low-value-added sectors
(agriculture, food) have delta near 1, tau_eff near 0, a near-zero
notch jump and a vanishing dominated region -- effectively no notch.
High-value-added services (delta near 0) face tau_eff near 0.20, the
full Kleven-Waseem £21,250 dominated region and the harsh notch. The
implicit uniform wedge HIDES exactly this heterogeneity.

CAVEATS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* delta is identified from sector aggregates via tau_eff = tau*(1-delta);
  net-repayment / zero-rated sectors give tau_eff~0 (delta clipped to <1)
  and high-VA sectors hit the data cap, so delta is clipped to [0,1).
* e is a calibration input, not identified from the synthetic data; all
  optima/bunchers are CONDITIONAL on the assumed e.
